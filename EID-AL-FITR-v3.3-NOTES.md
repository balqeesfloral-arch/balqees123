# Balqees Floral — Eid Al-Fitr v3.3

This build extends the approved luxury foundation and Ramadan v3.2 with a dedicated Eid Al-Fitr experience.

## Design direction
- Warm Saudi Eid morning mood rather than a recolored Ramadan theme.
- Real Balqees floral imagery inside an architectural portal.
- Restrained Islamic geometric patterning, warm champagne light and soft rose accents.
- Detailed skeuomorphic Eid cannon as the seasonal floating story trigger.
- On click: cannon recoil, muzzle flash, layered smoke and a short message-launch transition.
- Customer-facing message is a real Eid greeting rather than a description of the UI.

## Interaction
The cannon trigger toggles the Eid message. Clicking again closes it; clicking outside also closes it. Motion reduces automatically when prefers-reduced-motion is enabled.

## Reference rationale
The Eid cannon direction is grounded in documented Saudi Ramadan/Eid cannon tradition; visual execution remains original to Balqees.
